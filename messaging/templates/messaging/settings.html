Nobody: no one new can send you a message request. People whose conversation you already accepted can still reply.

Privacy

Others see two coloured ticks when you’ve read their message. If you turn this off, you also stop seeing when others have read yours.

Only people whose conversation you accepted can see it, never someone you blocked or who blocked you. If you turn this off, you also stop seeing when others are online.

Either person can change it in a chat; it applies to messages sent after the change.

What these settings can and can’t do

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest. Moderators can review messages only if they’re reported.
  • Disappearing messages and view-once photos are deleted from our servers when they expire, but the other person may already have seen, copied or photographed them, and copies can stay in backups until those backups expire.
  • Web browsers can’t block or detect screenshots or screen recording. In chats with disappearing messages we turn off text selection, and view-once photos are watermarked and blurred when you switch away. These only discourage copying; they don’t prevent it.
  • If you report a message, a copy is kept for the moderators until 180 days after the report is resolved, even if the message disappears.

Blocked

Blocked members can’t message you, can’t find you in search, and don’t see your profile card or photo.
