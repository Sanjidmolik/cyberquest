How your messages are protected
  • On the way: messages travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS, and WSS for live updates).
  • When stored: message text is encrypted in our database, with keys kept outside it.
  • Not end-to-end: CyberQuest’s servers can decrypt messages. Moderators read a message only when someone in the conversation reports it, and then only that message and up to three before and after it. Every time they open it, it is logged.
  • Automatic checks: before sending, we may ask “This message may be offensive. Edit or send anyway?”. That check never shows your message to anyone; we only count how often it appeared.
  • Don’t share passwords or other secrets in messages.
