How your messages are protected
  • Encrypted in transit and at rest. Moderators can review messages only if they’re reported.
  • Messages travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS, and WSS for live updates). Text and photos are encrypted in our database and storage, with keys kept outside it.
  • A moderator sees a message only when someone in the conversation reports it, and then only that message and up to three before and after it. Every time they open it, it is logged.
  • Disappearing messages and view-once photos are deleted from our servers when they expire. The other person may already have seen, copied or photographed them, and copies can stay in our backups until those backups expire.
  • Screenshots can’t be blocked in a web browser. Turning off copying, watermarks and blurring only discourage it.
  • Before sending, we may ask “This message may be offensive. Edit or send anyway?”. That check never shows your message to anyone; we only count how often it appeared.
