About CyberQuest

CyberQuest is a gamified cybersecurity learning platform built to teach real-world security awareness through hands-on simulation, not passive reading. Instead of memorizing rules, learners practice recognizing phishing emails, evaluating password strength, and defending against simulated attacks — earning experience points, leveling up, and unlocking achievement badges as they go.

The platform combines a structured course curriculum with interactive training games, so every concept taught is immediately reinforced through practice. Our goal is simple: make cybersecurity education engaging enough that people actually finish it, and effective enough that it sticks.

CyberQuest was built as a Computer Science & Engineering capstone project, with a focus on secure account practices, thoughtful gamification design, and a codebase built to grow — new courses and games can be added without reworking the platform's core.
